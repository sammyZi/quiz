# Science curriculum — for small children

Kid-friendly chapter. Short words. Big pictures. Same engine rule:
**nodes with something moving between them.**

Lesson IDs stay module-prefixed (`matter-001-ice-water-steam`).

Age feel: roughly **5–9** — curious, short attention, likes Ping the mascot.

---

## Engine constraint (same as CS)

If you can’t draw it as boxes + a moving label, it doesn’t belong here.

Good fits: ice → water → steam, sun → plant → leaf, push → ball → roll.  
Bad fits: long word lists, pure memorization with no motion.

*Nodes: ice / water / steam, mix bowls, objects in water. Motion: heat, bits mixing, sinking/floating.*

| ID | Lesson | Core diagram beat |

## Lesson count

**16 science lessons** in this subject.

Write order (engine-friendly first):
1. `matter-001` ice / water / steam — quality bar for kids science  
2. `force-001` push and pull  
3. `life-001` plants need sun  
4. Rest of Stuff around us, then Push and pull → Living things → Our world → Light and sound  

## Writing rules (kids)

- Hook: one short sentence a child can say aloud  
- Captions: under ~20 words when possible  
- Packet labels: 1–2 words (`heat`, `mix`, `fall`)  
- Quiz: 2–3 questions, silly wrong answers OK, never mean  
- No jargon (no “molecule”, “photosynthesis”, “precipitation”) unless the lesson *is* teaching a fun new word with a picture
